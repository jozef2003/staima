## What exactly does each agency sell and to whom?

### Takeaway
As of 2026, MYTY (Zurich/Berlin, founded 2020, buy-and-build with Ufenau Capital) has **about 12 active agency brands**. The group says it has "800+ specialists" and 25 locations in DE/CH/HR (+ Vienna and Paris through SESAMY). The portfolio has shrunk through mergers. INTEGR8, TRG and SAGA Digital were folded into norisk. Hutter Consult was folded into SiR MaRY MEDIA. appmotion was renamed SHAPE DACH. Yellow became "Strichpunkt Identity". The work mix is wide: branding/CI (Strichpunkt), e-commerce + performance (norisk), digital products/AI (SHAPE), creative + media (SiR MaRY), social content (monami), PR/public affairs/campaigning (CRK, Agence Trio, 404), and influencer/talent management (SESAMY, MOONGENCY, VYSION). Classic PR/public-affairs work sits mostly at CRK (CH), Agence Trio (CH-Romandie) and 404 (HR). The talent/influencer cluster is SESAMY, MOONGENCY and VYSION.

## What recurring work products do they produce (reporting, monitoring, content, pitches)?

### Takeaway
The recurring outputs are: ongoing social content and channel management (monami, 404, Trio, Hutter/SiR MaRY), influencer campaign management with reporting (MOONGENCY, SESAMY, 404), media plans and campaign performance reporting (SiR MaRY MEDIA, norisk/TRG), and PR/public-affairs work (CRK, 404, Trio). PR work here means media relations, crisis comms, election/referendum campaigns, association press offices and event/press trips. On top of that come new-business pitches (group-level BizDev boards) and branding guidelines and CI systems (Strichpunkt).
